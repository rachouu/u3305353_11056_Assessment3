Who Are We?

Canberra Modern is a volunteer led organisation dedicated to celebrating and preserving Canberra’s mid and late twentieth century architecture, landscapes, and urban design. Through curated tours, public programs, and educational initiatives, the organisation promotes awareness of Canberra’s unique modernist heritage.
