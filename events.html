Mid-Century Modern Walking Tour

24 August 2026 • Civic, Canberra

Join Canberra Modern for an immersive guided walking tour exploring Canberra’s iconic mid twentieth century architecture within the Civic precinct. Led by local heritage and architecture experts, the event will examine significant modernist buildings, urban planning principles, and the cultural history that shaped Canberra’s unique identity.

Participants will gain insight into how these structures contributed to the city’s development and why many of these places remain culturally significant today. The event encourages public engagement with heritage conservation while providing an accessible introduction to modernist design within Canberra.

Sunday 19 April 2026

Rivendell Revival

Welcome to Rivendell, designed in the late-twentieth century organic style of Laurie Virr as his own home in 1975. Hear from the house's new custodian, architect Shannon Battisson about the unique design of the house, Laurie Virr's incredible legacy and the recently completed large scale restoration of this significant piece of Canberra's design history. The ACT Heritage Library will provide a display of material from their fabulous archival collection which highlights the early solar passive design of Laurie Virr and another well known Canberra Architect - Derek Wrigley - early innovators and adopters of these principles in our city.
